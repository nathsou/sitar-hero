## Holst: *The Planets* (curated MIDI reductions)

These seven files derive from the [PDMX public-domain MIDI archive](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083/files/mid.tar.gz?download=1) ([dataset record and license details](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083)). PDMX recorded each source score's public-facing MuseScore license as [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and marked `license_conflict=False` against the score's internal license. PDMX converted the contributor scores to MIDI. We retained the source tempo and meter events, built a playable lead by selecting prominent pitched parts across each movement, and kept two or three accompaniment parts. When the main part rests for a long stretch, notes from another source part fill that gap. We omit those promoted notes from accompaniment to avoid doubling. In Neptune, held upper pitches of approximately 23, 32, and 46 seconds remain at full length in accompaniment while their lead copies are shortened, allowing the moving inner melody to appear in the game chart. These local files are modified arrangements, not byte-identical copies of the PDMX MIDIs. Each table entry identifies the exact path within `mid.tar.gz`, the original MuseScore page, the credited uploader, and all source MIDI track numbers used in the lead and accompaniment. Local track 0 carries tempo and meter, local track 1 is the playable lead, and the remaining tracks are accompaniment.
